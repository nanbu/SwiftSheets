# Code of conduct

SwiftSheets is a small project with a single maintainer, and the people who send issues and pull requests are what
make it better than one person could. The rules for talking to each other here are short.

## What is expected

- Be kind and assume good intent. Most people reporting a bug have just lost time to it.
- Talk about the code, the file and the behaviour, not about the person.
- Say what you verified and how. Disagreement is welcome when it comes with evidence.
- Respect a "no". A proposal that does not fit the scope in [CONTRIBUTING](CONTRIBUTING.md) is declined with a reason,
  not as a judgement of the person who made it.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity, background, experience or language.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or repeated disruption of a discussion.
- Publishing someone's private information, or a security issue before it is fixed (see [SECURITY](SECURITY.md)).
- Sexualised language or imagery.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to the repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and to anyone speaking for the
project elsewhere. Report a problem privately to the maintainer through the contact on the
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/nanbu), or through GitHub's own reporting tools. Reports are read in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock a thread, or block a participant who does not follow these rules,
and will say which rule was broken. The spirit follows the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/); where this page is silent, that is the guide.
